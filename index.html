03 Five Layers of a Personality

Five Layers,
One Code

A method that reads a person from every angle, then brings everything it sees together in one harmonious picture.

IColor · four schools, the 12-season system
IIFace · shape and visual balance
IIIBody & Proportion · cuts that suit the figure
IVStyling · pieces and details together
VVisual Identity · the whole picture, one language

I draw on the English, Italian, Brazilian and Canadian schools to read the undertone and place the season. With prints, I read the piece’s colors together, their contrast and their effect near the face.

Each client leaves knowing what suits her and why: the color near her face, her makeup shade, the printed piece.

Accreditation

Certified · Brazil Color Craft

I hold a certificate from Color Craft, Brazil, and am certified by them in color analysis. As one of their success partners, I market and distribute their seasonal color cards in Saudi Arabia and the Gulf, and use them in my consultations as a practical reference the client carries when she shops.

Paper · In Progress Arab and Saudi skin

I am preparing a paper on how well foreign color analysis methods fit Arab skin, with a particular focus on Saudi skin. Its question comes from my practice: how far do these methods account for the range of skin tones and undertones here?

12Case Study · Oriental Fashion Show

Clothing designed around the needs of women with scoliosis

My work with an influencer covers where she appears, why that appearance suits her and which design tells her story. This project brings several of my roles together, before and after the show photographs.

I
Opening the door for two designersI helped two designers who developed clothing for women with scoliosis present their project at the Oriental Fashion Show.
II
Choosing the influencer and the designI brought an influencer to walk as a model and, as her stylist, chose the design that tells the project’s story and fits her appearance.
III
Styling the whole lookI set the direction for her makeup and hair to serve her and the design, and brought in the beauty team.
IV
Taking the story to the publicI arranged an interview for the designers with the channel Ma Qal Wa Dal, hosted the filming at the IMAGE CODE studio and shared the project on my channels.
